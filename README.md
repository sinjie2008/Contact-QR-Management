# Contact QR Management

Standalone test site for managing contact QR codes.

## Features
- Search, sorting, status filter and pagination
- Add / Edit / Remove contacts
- CSV import and matching CSV template download
- Official WhatsApp QR image support
- WeChat QR
- Full V-card QR with .VCF download
- Click any QR to open a large testing preview
- Fully populated built-in contact data
- Missing required QR values display **N/A**
- Browser localStorage persistence and Reset Test Data

## Run
Open `index.html` in a browser. The page uses QRCode.js from cdnjs for generated V-card / test QR codes.

## QR behavior

### WhatsApp QR
WhatsApp's **Add via QR code** scanner only accepts an official WhatsApp contact QR generated by WhatsApp.

Get it from WhatsApp:
1. Open WhatsApp.
2. Open Settings / your profile.
3. Tap the QR icon next to your name.
4. Open **My Code**.
5. Share or save the QR image.
6. Upload that image in this site, or provide its image URL.

If no official WhatsApp QR image is supplied, the WhatsApp QR column displays **N/A**.

The site intentionally does **not** generate a generic vCard or `wa.me` QR in the WhatsApp QR column because WhatsApp's dedicated Add-via-QR scanner rejects those.

### V-card QR
The V-card QR is generated from the form fields and is the generic **Add Contact** QR. Scan it using the normal iPhone / Android camera to create or save the contact.

### WeChat QR
WeChat QR requires a WeChat ID for the test QR, or an official uploaded WeChat QR image for production use.
